- **SBR Builder** (Asman Oky ES): a TurkmenStat platform for reports, payments and invoices, used by 100+ staff. Built with Laravel and Nuxt.js, plus a reusable Tailwind component library.
- **Capacity planning** (VERO Digital Solutions): I was the sole developer of live crew allocation across construction sites. It replaced planning spreadsheets and catches over- and under-staffed sites before they cause delays.
- **E-commerce for jewelry retail** (AMPTIVE): checkout, payment gateways, inventory and B2B wholesale ordering for 10+ US and Canadian stores. I also moved legacy jQuery frontends to Vue.js.
- **[DaiDengi](https://github.com/rovshena/loan-app)** (freelance): a loan-listings marketplace with accounts, moderation and search filters.
